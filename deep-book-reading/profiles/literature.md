# Literature profile

Prioritize plot structure, character development, relationships, narrator/reliability, point of view, setting, imagery, motif, language, genre, theme, and ambiguity. Preserve passages' form and context when analysis depends on them.

Do not force literary insight into procedural knowledge. Knowledge units may represent concepts, claims about interpretation, patterns, cases, counterexamples, and limitations, but the Skillability veto normally rejects themes or character lessons that lack defined repeatable inputs, decisions, and outputs.
