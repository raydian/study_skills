# Business and management profile

Prioritize Problem → Observation → Framework → Method → Decision rule → Case → Boundary. Separate memorable cases from general evidence; anecdotes rarely establish generality.

Read diagrams, matrices, scorecards, and tables as potential framework definitions. Critical reading should test selection bias, survivorship bias, causality, organizational context, incentives, and whether a historical success case transfers. Generated Skills must preserve context and contraindications.
