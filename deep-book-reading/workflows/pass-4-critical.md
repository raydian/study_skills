# PASS 4 — Critical reading

Evaluate the strongest and most consequential claims first.

## Lenses

- assumptions and definitions;
- evidence quality, directness, replication, and representativeness;
- correlation, mechanism, and causation;
- sampling and selection effects;
- alternative explanations and rival theories;
- counterexamples and falsifying conditions;
- historical, cultural, technical, or organizational scope;
- time sensitivity and current applicability;
- gap between cases and general rules.

For each critical unit, present four separately labeled elements: Author View, Evidence, Alternative View, AI Assessment. Use `critical_analysis` attribution for the assessment and preserve the source references being evaluated. External facts require their own sources and cannot be smuggled into the book's evidence base.

## Exit gate

All high-impact claims have an evidence-strength judgment and boundary review. Unsupported criticism is removed or labeled as an open question.
