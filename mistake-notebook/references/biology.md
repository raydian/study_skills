## Subject-Specific Mistake Causes

- 概念边界混淆、结构功能对应错误。
- 生命活动过程顺序错误、调节机制因果链错误。
- 遗传概率计算错误、系谱图判断错误。
- 实验变量、对照组、结论表达错误。
- 图表信息提取不全或曲线趋势误读。

## Requirements

- Separate facts from reasoning conclusions.
- For experiments, identify independent variable, dependent variable, control, and conclusion.
- Same-type variants should test process reasoning or evidence extraction.
