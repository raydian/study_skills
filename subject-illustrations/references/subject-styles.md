# Subject Style Constraints

Use one consistent style per subject. Add the selected style text to every image prompt for that subject. Across all subjects, prioritize knowledge structure diagrams, relationship maps, process diagrams, method paths, microscopic mechanisms, real-world operation diagrams, and evidence/answer pathways. Use ordinary “配图” scenes only as supporting context.

## 通用生成原则

- Main image role: explain knowledge, not decorate the page.
- Preferred forms: knowledge structure map, concept relationship map, method flowchart, comparison diagram, cause-effect chain, process timeline, microscopic mechanism diagram, real-world operation mechanism, experiment/device diagram, text-structure map, problem-solving path.
- Required clarity: use labels, arrows, hierarchy, sequence numbers, color meaning, key variables, and final conclusion where useful.
- Style consistency: keep a stable background, palette, line thickness, label style, and diagram language within one subject.
- Avoid: title-only posters, generic scenes without explanatory labels, photorealistic clutter, dark cinematic style, decorative gradients, and unrelated icons.

## 数学

Use precise mathematical board-diagram style: white or light grid background, black/deep-blue lines, orange highlights for key conditions, transformations, and conclusions. Prefer knowledge maps, formula relation diagrams, set/Venn diagrams, number lines, coordinate systems, function graphs, geometric construction diagrams, proof-flow diagrams, and problem-solving pathways. Show “definition -> condition -> equivalent form -> method -> conclusion” whenever possible. Use LaTeX-like symbols and exact labels. Real-world scenes are secondary and should appear only when modeling a problem; they must connect to variables, equations, or diagrams. Avoid decorative math posters, inaccurate graphs, and stylized 3D objects.

## 物理

Use clean physics textbook diagram style: white or light grid background, deep-blue/black object outlines, orange vectors/arrows for force, velocity, acceleration, displacement, field direction, energy transfer, or process direction. Prefer process diagrams, motion-state diagrams, force analysis diagrams, real-world operation mechanism diagrams, device/experiment schematics, graph-reading diagrams, and model-building paths. Show “physical situation -> object/system -> forces/quantities -> law/model -> result” clearly. Use real-world scenes only when they expose the operating mechanism, such as elevator motion, braking, projectile motion, circuits, or wave propagation, with labels and arrows. Avoid cinematic photos, unlabeled objects, ambiguous arrows, and decorative realism.

## 化学

Use clean chemistry textbook diagram style: white background, precise apparatus drawings, particle/molecular diagrams, reaction pathway arrows, ion/electron-transfer arrows, and observation labels. Prefer knowledge structure maps, reaction classification diagrams, experiment-operation diagrams, microscopic particle views, electron transfer diagrams, ion migration diagrams, and “macro phenomenon -> micro particle -> symbol equation” three-level diagrams. Show real-world operation when relevant, such as purification, corrosion, electrolysis, material production, or environmental cycles, but connect it to substances, particles, equations, and observations. Use orange highlights for electron movement, key reactants/products, precipitate, gas, heat, color change, or active sites. Avoid decorative lab scenes, photorealistic clutter, and unlabelled beakers.

## 生物

Use biology textbook diagram style: clean white background, simplified accurate structures, layered labels, arrows for material flow, signal flow, energy flow, genetic information flow, evolution, or process stages. Prefer structure-function maps, life-process flowcharts, cellular/microscopic mechanism diagrams, organ-system relationship diagrams, ecological flow diagrams, experiment evidence chains, and comparison diagrams. Show “structure -> function -> process -> regulation -> result” where possible. Use microscopic views for cells, organelles, membranes, genes, enzymes, and tissues; use real-world/ecosystem operation views for population, community, homeostasis, and human health topics. Avoid cute cartoon organisms, overly realistic medical imagery, and unlabeled anatomical pictures.

## 地理

Use geography textbook map and process-diagram style: clear maps, cross-sections, flow arrows, climate/terrain symbols, scale cues, and labeled spatial relationships. Prefer spatial structure maps, cause-effect chains, regional element relationship maps, physical process diagrams, human-land interaction diagrams, flow maps, profile diagrams, and decision/management diagrams. Show “location -> natural/human elements -> process/mechanism -> spatial pattern -> impact/management” clearly. Real-world operation diagrams are important for topics like water cycle, monsoon, urbanization, agriculture, industry, transportation, hazards, and resource management. Use muted map colors, deep-blue outlines, and orange highlights for routes, boundaries, gradients, key nodes, or management actions. Avoid travel-poster scenery and decorative maps.

## 历史

Use historical structure and source-analysis diagram style: white or light parchment background, timelines, cause-effect arrows, comparison columns, geographic markers, actor relationship maps, institution diagrams, and evidence chains. Prefer chronology maps, event-cause-result diagrams, political/economic/social/cultural structure charts, reform/war/revolution process diagrams, source interpretation paths, and “background -> process -> result -> influence” knowledge maps. Real-world operation views should show how systems worked, such as taxation, bureaucracy, trade networks, military mobilization, diplomacy, or social organization. Use restrained colors, deep-blue/black text, and orange highlights for turning points, causal links, key actors, or institutional mechanisms. Avoid dramatic cinematic battle scenes unless labels and structure explain the historical point.

## 语文

Use text-structure and reading-analysis diagram style: white or light paper background, paragraph maps, argument-flow diagrams, character relationship networks, imagery/motif webs, emotional-change curves, narrative structure diagrams, and rhetoric/effect cards. Prefer diagrams that explain “文本内容 -> 结构层次 -> 表达手法 -> 情感/主旨 -> 答题角度”. For poetry and prose, show imagery relations, sensory layers, spatial/temporal movement, and emotion shifts. For argument and practical texts, show claim-evidence-reasoning structure. Contextual scenes should be secondary and must support textual interpretation, not become cover art. Use deep-blue lines/text and orange highlights for key sentences, emotional turns, rhetorical devices, or evidence points.

## 英语

Use language-learning diagram style: clean white background, grammar trees, sentence pattern boxes, discourse flow arrows, vocabulary mind maps, reading evidence maps, and writing-structure templates. Prefer diagrams that explain “form -> meaning -> usage -> example -> common error” for grammar and vocabulary, and “question -> locate evidence -> infer -> answer” for reading. Use real-world situational scenes only when they clarify communication context, and add labels for roles, expressions, intentions, and discourse markers. Use deep-blue text/lines and orange highlights for tense markers, clauses, signal words, collocations, answer evidence, or writing transitions. Avoid cartoon scenes that do not teach language structure.
